# SOUND OF SPACE — SCIENTIFIC SOURCES & MEDIA ATTRIBUTIONS

All scientific data, orbital parameters, physical constants, historical mission profiles, and electromagnetic sonification principles used in **SOUND OF SPACE** are strictly grounded in official peer-reviewed astronomical research and public planetary science repositories.

---

## 1. Planetary & Celestial Physics Data

- **NASA Planetary Fact Sheet**:
  - Author: Dr. David R. Williams, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center (GSFC)
  - Source: [https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/)
  - Utilized for: Planet diameters, mean densities, masses, surface gravities, escape velocities, rotation periods, orbital periods, perihelion/aphelion distances, mean temperatures, and atmospheric pressure values.

- **JPL Solar System Dynamics (SSD)**:
  - Source: Jet Propulsion Laboratory, California Institute of Technology (Caltech) / NASA
  - URL: [https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/)
  - Utilized for: Keplerian orbital elements, planetary mass ratios, and natural satellite orbital distances.

- **IAU (International Astronomical Union)**:
  - Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature & Working Group for Planetary System Nomenclature (WGPSN)
  - Utilized for: Recognized moon counts, surface feature naming conventions (Olympus Mons, Valles Marineris, Great Red Spot, Sputnik Planitia).

## 2. Cosmic Audio & Sonification Data

- **Solar Helioseismology**:
  - Sources: SOHO (Solar and Heliospheric Observatory) Michelson Doppler Imager (MDI), NASA SDO (Solar Dynamics Observatory) HMI, and Stanford University Solar Center.
  - Method: Acoustic p-mode pressure wave frequencies (~3 mHz) transposed into the audible human hearing range (80–240 Hz).

- **Jupiter Decametric Radio (DAM) & Plasma Waves**:
  - Sources: NASA Voyager 1/2 Plasma Wave Subsystem (PWS) and NASA Juno Waves Instrument Team (University of Iowa).
  - Method: Electromagnetic cyclotron radio bursts emitted by electrons in Jupiter’s magnetosphere near the Io plasma torus, converted from radio signals to audio.

- **Saturn Kilometric Radiation (SKR)**:
  - Source: Cassini Radio and Plasma Wave Science (RPWS) Team, NASA / ESA / ASI.
  - Method: Auroral radio emissions modulated by Saturn's rotation period, transposed into the audible band.

- **Earth Magnetospheric Chorus & Whistlers**:
  - Source: NASA Van Allen Probes EMFISIS (Electric and Magnetic Field Instrument Suite and Integrated Science) Team.
  - Method: Direct conversion of natural electromagnetic plasma chorus waves (already vibrating between 300 Hz and 4,000 Hz) into audio.

- **Gravitational Wave Sonification**:
  - Source: LIGO (Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory) & Virgo Scientific Collaboration (Caltech / MIT).
  - Method: Strain data $h(t)$ from event GW150914 (binary black hole merger) translated into sound frequencies (35 Hz to 280 Hz).

- **Pulsar Radio Emissions**:
  - Source: Jodrell Bank Centre for Astrophysics (University of Manchester) & Australia Telescope National Facility (ATNF) Pulsar Catalogue.
  - Method: Periodic radio pulses of the Crab Pulsar (PSR B0531+21, ~29.7 Hz) rendered at its physical pulse repetition frequency.

  - URL: [https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/missions/](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/missions/)
